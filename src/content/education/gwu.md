Finance and economics, then a fairly straight line into credit analysis at
Loews. CFA Level I alongside it.

The part that stuck was not the curriculum. It was the habit of not accepting a
number until you know how it was built, which turned out to be the most
transferable thing I took out of undergrad.

Nearly every product argument I have been in came down to numbers. Whether a
metric measures what someone says it does. Whether a projection holds. Whether
a cost saving is real or an accounting artifact. Being able to check the
arithmetic yourself is a quiet advantage, and it is why I am careful about the
figures on this site.
