Three and a half years of core product management, mostly in travel and
hospitality, mostly on systems with a lot of users and a lot of history.

The through-line was replatforming and tooling: taking something that worked
but didn't scale — a loyalty program on aging infrastructure, a website
creation process that took weeks per property — and rebuilding it around what
the people using it actually needed.

This is where I learned requirements at volume. Four hundred of them on one
program, gathered across twenty-five discovery sessions with people whose
incentives did not align. Deloitte sponsored my MBA on the strength of this
work, and I returned to the firm afterward.
