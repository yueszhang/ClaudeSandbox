My first job after graduating. Outsourced CIO work, which meant sitting on the
allocator side rather than the issuer side.

The work split between manager selection and client service. I ran due
diligence on hedge fund investments, largely through investor conferences and
capital introduction meetings, and the ones I recommended outperformed the HFR
indices. Alongside that I monitored and traded client portfolios against their
investment policies and tactical tilts, and prepared the reports and market
summaries that went out to them.

The piece I found most interesting was benchmark construction. Building
client-specific target benchmarks across every asset class meant reconciling
asset class tilts with ESG mandates and legal constraints, and no two clients
wanted the same thing. It was the first time I had to turn a set of conflicting
stated requirements into one coherent specification, which is most of what I do
now.
