I started in finance. Recommended new issues and relative value trades across a
$750M aerospace and defense book, briefing portfolio managers on sentiment,
industry headwinds, and market structure.

The pivot happened sideways. The desk needed better portfolio analytics, I
volunteered to run it, and I spent months interviewing traders and research
analysts and turning what they said into user stories and requirements. It
shipped to 120+ people.

That project was more interesting to me than the trades. I have been doing some
version of it ever since — the analytical training just stayed useful, because
most product arguments are ultimately arguments about numbers.
