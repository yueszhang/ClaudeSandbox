I started in finance. New issues and relative value trades across a $750M
aerospace and defense book, briefing portfolio managers on sentiment, industry
headwinds and market structure.

The pivot happened sideways. The desk needed better portfolio analytics, I put
my hand up, and I spent months interviewing traders and research analysts and
turning what they told me into requirements. It shipped to 120-plus people.

That project held my attention in a way the trades did not, and I have been
doing some version of it since. The analytical training stayed useful because
most product arguments turn out to be arguments about numbers.
